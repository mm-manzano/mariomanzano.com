# Mario Manzano — Premium Redesign: Design Ideas

## Approach A — Quiet Luxury Editorial

**Design Movement:** Quiet Luxury / Architectural Digest editorial

**Core Principles:**
- Extreme whitespace as a signal of confidence and restraint
- Typography does the heavy lifting — no decorative noise
- Photography is the hero, not a background
- Every CTA feels like an invitation, not a demand

**Color Philosophy:** Warm off-white (#F8F5F0) base, deep charcoal (#1A1A18) text, single gold accent (#B8974A) used sparingly. Emotional intent: calm authority, old-money restraint.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial columns. Left-heavy text blocks anchored by full-bleed right-side imagery. Sections break the grid intentionally.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rules as section dividers
- Large numerals (01, 02, 03) as section markers
- Oversized pull-quotes in light italic serif

**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, deliberate hover reveals. No aggressive popups. Scroll-triggered fade-ins that feel like turning a magazine page.

**Animation:** Subtle opacity transitions (300ms ease-out). No bouncing or scaling. Text slides in from left on scroll.

**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (display/headlines, 400/300 weight) + DM Sans (body, 400/500). Headlines at 72–96px on desktop.

## Approach B — Modern Brutalist Luxury

**Design Movement:** Brutalist editorial meets luxury real estate

**Core Principles:**
- Bold typographic statements dominate every section
- Stark contrast between black and warm cream
- Structured grid with intentional rule-breaking
- Confidence through simplicity and scale

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#111111), warm cream (#F2EDE4), and a single copper accent (#C4874A). Intent: bold, modern, unapologetic.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width text banners alternating with edge-to-edge photography. No centered layouts. Text bleeds to edges.

**Signature Elements:**
- Massive weight-contrast typography (thin + ultra-bold on same line)
- Full-width horizontal photo strips
- Stark black footer with minimal white text

**Interaction Philosophy:** Immediate, high-contrast hover states. Bold underlines on links. No subtle animations — everything is intentional and direct.

**Animation:** Fast snap transitions (150ms). Horizontal text marquees for social proof. Parallax on hero image.

**Typography System:** Playfair Display (headlines, 700/900) + Outfit (body, 300/400). Extreme size contrast between H1 and body.

## Approach C — Warm Texas Modernism

**Design Movement:** Texas Hill Country modernism — organic warmth meets clean architecture

**Core Principles:**
- Warmth and approachability without sacrificing premium feel
- Nature-inspired textures and tones
- Generous breathing room with layered depth
- Storytelling through imagery and honest copy

**Color Philosophy:** Warm sand (#E8DDD0), deep cedar (#2C2218), sage green (#7A8C6E), and aged brass (#A8834A). Intent: grounded, trustworthy, Texas-rooted.

**Layout Paradigm:** Staggered card grid with overlapping elements. Sections have slight diagonal cuts. Content flows organically rather than in rigid columns.

**Signature Elements:**
- Organic texture overlays (subtle grain on hero sections)
- Staggered image-text pairs that alternate sides
- Hand-drawn style divider lines

**Interaction Philosophy:** Warm, inviting hover states with soft color fills. Forms feel conversational. CTAs use action-oriented but friendly language.

**Animation:** Gentle upward float on scroll (400ms ease). Warm color transitions on hover. Subtle parallax on hero.

**Typography System:** Lora (headlines, 400/600 italic) + Source Sans 3 (body, 400/600). Mid-scale headlines at 48–72px.

---

## Selected Approach: **A — Quiet Luxury Editorial**

Chosen because it most precisely matches the brand positioning: a calm, knowledgeable advisor who helps homeowners make thoughtful decisions. The restraint of Quiet Luxury signals expertise without shouting. The editorial layout creates a premium feel that differentiates Mario from typical "agent websites." Cormorant Garamond headlines will feel sophisticated and distinctive without being cold.
